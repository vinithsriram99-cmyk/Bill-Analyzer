# 4-minute demo video script (record with QuickTime: File > New Screen Recording)

1. **0:00 Problem (20 s).** "Government bills are written in legal language, so most citizens cannot tell what they change. PPIIA turns a bill into a plain-language analysis."
2. **0:20 Input (20 s).** Show the three sources: PDF upload, URL, pasted text, sample bill. Choose a sample bill and click **Analyze bill**.
3. **0:40 Summary tab (40 s).** Read two summary lines, point to objective, key provisions, positives and negatives.
4. **1:20 Industry impact (30 s).** Show the sector chart (rule-based classifier) and the Benefit / Restriction table (Gemini).
5. **1:50 Timeline + Dependency graph (40 s).** "These are the earlier laws, amendments and committees the bill connects to, found by pattern matching."
6. **2:30 Impact forecast and risks (40 s).** Short / medium / long term; clause-level risk flags with real sentences from the bill.
7. **3:10 Download (20 s).** Download the JSON and the PDF summary and open the PDF.
8. **3:30 Honesty and design (30 s).** "The summary is AI inference, not legal advice. If Gemini is unavailable the app falls back to an offline extractive summary. One Gemini call per bill keeps it inside the free tier."
